# Arizona Bean Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 - 14 oz. can lima beans
- 1 - 14 oz. can kidney beans
- 1 - 14 oz. can french style green beans
- 1 - 28 oz. can pork & beans
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup Heinz chili sauce
- 1 bunch green onions (chopped)
- 1 red bell pepper (sliced)
- 5 slices of bacon

## Instructions
1. **Fry bacon in a large skillet until crisp.**
    Leave the grease in the pan, then add the chopped green onions and sliced red bell pepper. Cook until tender.
2. **Stir in brown sugar and chili sauce.**
    Simmer over medium heat until the sugar has melted and the sauce is well combined.
3. **Drain the lima beans, kidney beans, and green beans (do not drain the pork & beans).**
    In a large casserole dish or crock pot, combine all the beans. Pour the sauce and vegetable mixture over the beans and mix well. Break the bacon into pieces and sprinkle on top.
4. **Bake at 350°F for 1 hour.**
    Serve hot.
