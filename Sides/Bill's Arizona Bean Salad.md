# Arizona Bean Salad

## Ingredients:
- 14oz can of lima beans
- 14oz can of kidney beans
- 14oz can French-style green beans
- 28oz can pork & beans
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup Heinz chili sauce
- 1 bunch scallions (green onion), chopped
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 5-6 slices of bacon

## Cooking:
- Fry bacon in a pan until crispy
- Remove bacon, leaving grease behind in the pan
- Add sliced peppers and scallions and fry until tender
- Add brown sugar and chili sauce. Stir and simmer until sugar has melted, then set aside
- Open and strain all cans EXCEPT the pork & beans
- Combine all beans in a crock pot or large casserole dish with a lid
- Crumble or chop bacon into pieces and add
- Add sauce to beans and bacon and mix well
- Bake at 350ºF for 1 hour
  - Alternatively: cook in crockpot on high for ~3-4 hours, or low for ~6-8 hours
